# BetBanish product context

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

People using Chrome who want a pause before visiting gambling sites. BetBanish gives them time to reconsider and a choice to leave or continue. Existing users and their saved site settings take priority in this update. The README and approved roadmap define the product as a nudge, with no hard blocking or accounts.

## Voice and principles

Keep the existing plain, brief, nonjudgmental voice. Make leaving easy, keep the choice to continue, and describe spending projections accurately. Preserve the privacy promise: no analytics or backend; personal reminders stay on the device. Site coverage should be understandable and easy to configure. Avoid streaks or savings claims that cannot be established from browser activity.

## Accessibility and presentation

Retain the existing system typography, compact settings forms, light surfaces, and clear button shapes. New controls must have visible labels and keyboard focus. The overlay must keep its actions reachable on short windows and at increased zoom. No decorative animation is needed.
